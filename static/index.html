"Reclaiming Attention"

A multimodal exploration of art in the age of overstimulation

Overstimulation Room: Enter a chaotic environment filled with fleeting, distorted images and simultaneous digital notifications, simulating the sensory overload and frustration of modern hyper-connectivity.

Isolation Room: Navigate through a completely dark space guided only by a rope, accompanied by unsettling sounds that evoke feelings of loneliness and the fear of missing out.

Reconnection Room: Find relief in a calm, welcoming environment where you can collaborate with others to assemble a digital puzzle, encouraging shared focus and meaningful interaction.
